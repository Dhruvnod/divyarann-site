A note from the author

Namaste to all my fellow readers. This book is my dream project: a painting that never had a canvas to be painted on. My aim with this story is to tell people about our culture and history in my own fictional way. I want to share my own learnings, discoveries and interests in Sanatan Dharma, and how deeply it has influenced my life. The book uses many terms from our sacred Vedas, our scriptures and our beloved stories.

I would also like to sincerely thank everyone who supports me. No human being can achieve anything alone in this world. As the saying goes, "Behind every successful man there is a woman," and that is true in my case as well. I have three beautiful and amazing women in my life, to whom I owe everything.

My mother, who brought me into this beautiful world and has looked after me ever since.

My sister, who supports me in everything I do and is always there for me.

And finally, my girlfriend, who pulled me out of a very dark place and has loved and supported me since the day we met. I owe her the most!

Once again, I thank all my readers from the bottom of my heart.
