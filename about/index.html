The book

DIVYARANN is a trilogy of Indian mythological fantasy set in modern Delhi. Book One, Vinash, is published here as a free web edition, one chapter at a time, while it is being written. Chapters may be revised, and this edition may be withdrawn when the book is published.

The site grows as you read: the summaries, the codex, the map and the wheel open chapter by chapter, for every reader, whenever they start.

The author

Written by Dhruv. To get in touch, write to hello@divyarann.com or find the book on Instagram.

Privacy

Your reading progress stays on your device. Which chapters you have read, where you stopped, your reading settings and what has unsealed for you are kept in your browser’s own storage on this phone or computer. They are not sent to us or anyone else, and clearing your browser’s site data resets them.

No tracking. The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

Email. If you sign up for chapter emails, your address is kept by Kit, the mail service, only to send you new chapters. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages; both see the usual technical details of a web request, such as your IP address.
