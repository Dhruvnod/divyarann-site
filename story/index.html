The Story

Two months before the sky opens, seismometers around the world start recording earthquakes that are not happening. In Dwarka, a shopkeeper with a debt and an engineer with a fever feel the pulses in their bodies before anyone can explain them. Then seven tears open in the sky, one on each continent, and the things that come through wear the shapes of the oldest stories.
